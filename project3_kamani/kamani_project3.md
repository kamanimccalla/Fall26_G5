## Section 1: Feature

### Create and Track Study Goals

The goal-setting feature allows a student to select a course, set a target number of study hours, and choose a deadline. After the student creates a goal, the application displays the completed hours and the remaining study time. Each time the student logs a study session, the application updates the goal's progress.

The feature includes the following functions:

- Create a goal for a specific course.
- Enter the target number of study hours.
- Select a deadline.
- Log completed study sessions.
- View completed hours, remaining hours, and goal status.

## Section 2: Persona

### Jordan, a Busy Undergraduate Student

Jordan is a 20-year-old undergraduate student who takes five courses and works part time. Jordan sometimes waits until the last minute to study because assignments, work, and personal responsibilities compete for attention. Jordan needs a simple way to set goals for different courses and determine whether enough study time has been completed before an exam or assignment deadline.

**Goals:**

- Organize study time by course.
- Begin studying before the last minute.
- Monitor progress toward weekly or exam-related targets.

**Challenges:**

- Balancing school with a part-time job.
- Remembering deadlines for several courses.
- Knowing how much additional study time is needed.

## Section 3: Scenario

### Preparing for a Mathematics Exam

Jordan has a mathematics exam in two weeks and wants to study for five hours before the exam. Jordan opens the Study Habit Tracker and selects **Create Goal**. Jordan enters Mathematics as the course, sets a target of five study hours, and chooses the exam date as the deadline.

After each study session, Jordan records the amount of time spent studying. The application updates the goal and displays the completed hours, the remaining hours, and whether the goal is on track. Jordan checks this information throughout the two weeks and completes the five-hour target before the exam.

## Section 4: User Story

> As a busy college student, I want to create a study goal with a course, target number of hours, and deadline so that I can organize my study time and monitor my progress.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. The student can enter a course name.
2. The student can enter a target number of study hours.
3. The student can choose a deadline.
4. The student can save the goal.
5. The application displays completed and remaining study hours.
6. Logged study sessions update the goal's progress.
